# Mission statement — the captain's words, 2026-09-12

A note, not a specification. Recorded verbatim because it was stated as the thing docstrap is for, and
because it names an audience the specification's §0 does not.

> docstrap mission is to discover, organize, and manage documents used as specs, prompts, user
> documentation, and development records and optimize there format and delivery for both human
> developers and agents. Docstrap eases comunication between actors and integrates useful tools to
> annotate, and colaborate.

## What it adds to what §0 already says

`docs/docstrap_on_2026-09-01.md` §0 describes the mechanism — discovery through `.docstrap`, Sphinx rendering, the two
databases, tools layering apparatus into the page. It does not state who the output is for, and that is
where this sits rather than replacing anything.

Two things in the statement are not derivable from §0.

1. **The audience is plural and one half of it is machine.** Format and delivery are to be optimised for
   human developers *and* agents. §0 is silent on agents; the front-matter reader and the answer store
   serve them in practice, but nothing says so as an aim.
2. **The document classes are named.** Specs, prompts, user documentation, and development records. §0
   says "documentation projects" throughout, which covers user documentation comfortably and the other
   three only by implication. Prompts in particular are promdance's material, and this statement puts
   them inside the mission rather than beside it.

The third clause — easing communication between actors, and integrating tools to annotate and
collaborate — is consistent with §7's apparatus and §6's annotation store, and reads as the purpose
those were built toward.

## Status

Held here deliberately. The captain's own view is that it may be weaker than the text already in the
specification, so nothing in §0 was changed. If any of it should bind, it is promoted from this note
into the specification by his decision, not by a document's claim.
